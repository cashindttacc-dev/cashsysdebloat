# CASH Theme Gallery Upgrade

- The sun icon is the dedicated theme button.
- Clicking it opens a scrollable two-column CASH Theme Gallery.
- CASH Black & Purple is the signature theme and is the default when no preference exists.
- Emulator Optimization is explicitly visible between Tweaks and Win11 Creator.
- The compiled `winutil.ps1` and source `xaml/inputXML.xaml` are kept in sync.
